## Product Purpose
MirrorMe is a choreography comparison tool for dance captains and dancers. It compares a dancer submission against a reference video using computer vision and provides timestamped feedback, severity labels, and a score that supports faster iteration.

## Users
Primary user: dance captains managing weekly team assignments and feedback.
Secondary user: dancers submitting videos and reviewing corrections.

## Tone
Clear, encouraging, confident, and practical.

## Strategic Principles
- Preserve trust in scoring by keeping alignment and analysis transparent.
- Keep workflows fast for weekly team use.
- Favor intuitive controls and visual clarity over decorative complexity.
- Design should feel performance/studio inspired, but still professional and readable.

## Anti-References
- Generic dark SaaS dashboards with neon accents.
- Overly minimal interfaces that hide key actions.
- Over-animated UI that distracts from review tasks.
