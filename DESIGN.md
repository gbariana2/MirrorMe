# DESIGN

## Direction
Light, dance-studio inspired interface with warm neutrals and expressive rose/coral accents. Prioritize readability and fast task completion.

## Color Strategy
Restrained-to-committed product palette:
- Warm paper background
- Soft cream surfaces
- Rose accent for navigation and labels
- Coral accent for primary CTAs
- Semantic success/warning/error tones remain clear and accessible

## Typography
Use a legible sans stack with confident hierarchy:
- Strong title contrast
- Compact but readable body copy
- Clear labels and status text

## Components
- Rounded panels with subtle elevation
- Visible hover/focus states
- Filters and status controls as clear chips
- Minimal motion, mostly interaction feedback

## Brand Mark
MirrorMe logo uses two mirrored Ms side-by-side to represent reflection/comparison.
